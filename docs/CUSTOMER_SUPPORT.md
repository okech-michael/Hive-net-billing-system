# Customer support

For a delayed payment, ask the customer to check the Safaricom prompt and confirm the phone number and time. Search the payment order by the order reference through the database/API logs; do not ask for or record an M-Pesa PIN. An order remains pending until provider reconciliation succeeds.

An entitlement may be confirmed while network activation stays pending because no router integration is enabled in this version. Do not claim the customer is online; escalate for manual, audited network handling. SMS delivery, access-code recovery and customer support ticket workflows are not yet available.
